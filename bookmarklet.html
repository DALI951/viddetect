YouTube Mix → viddetect

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any YouTube mix page to send the exact playlist to viddetect.

↗ Send to viddetect
  1. Drag the button above to your browser's bookmarks bar
  2. Go to YouTube and open any mix or playlist page
  3. Click the bookmarklet — it reads the playlist data and sends it to viddetect
  4. You'll see an alert: Sent N videos to viddetect!
  5. Go back to viddetect and click "Browse" on the playlist — the entries will be exactly what YouTube showed you
The data stays cached for 24 hours. Run the bookmarklet again on the same mix to refresh it.
